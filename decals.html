Custom Decals & Stickers

Put your brand on everything. We generate thick, waterproof vinyl decals, multi-sticker sheets, storefront vinyl lettering, and heavy-duty car window decals.

Description

Weatherproof Vinyl Plotter Graphics

Whether you want custom die-cut stickers to promote your brand or cut vinyl lettering to showcase your business hours on storefront glass, decals are a versatile marketing asset. We output sticker designs onto thick, premium vinyl using wide-format digital presses.

To ensure complete weatherproofing, every sticker receives a clear **UV Matte or Gloss Lamination Overlay**. This protects the ink pigments from sun exposure, friction, and moisture, preventing fading and scratching. We offer **Die-Cut** options (where the backing is cut to the exact shape of the graphic) and **Kiss-Cut** options (perfect for sticker sheets).

Sticker & Decal Options:

  • Custom Die-Cut Stickers: Cut to the custom outline of your graphic with standard thick borders.
  • Multi-Sticker Sheets: Multiple kiss-cut decals mapped on a single standard backing sheet.
  • Transfer Storefront Lettering: Plotter cut colored vinyl pre-masked with transfer tape.

Vinyl Decal Specifications

Material Classifications

  • 4mil Weatherproof Vinyl: Thick, flexible PVC substrate base. Resists shrinking and bubbling.
  • UV Protective Laminate: 3mil protective clear shield layer, available in Glossy or Satin Matte finishes.
  • Eco-Solvent Inks: High-saturation pigments matching wide color spreads.
  • Standard Turnaround: 4 to 6 Business Days from graphic approval.

Cut Terminology

  • Die-Cut: Standard punch cut through the vinyl and backing paper, matching the exact sticker shape.
  • Kiss-Cut: Cut through the vinyl layer only, leaving the backing paper intact as a rectangular carrier.
  • Transfer Decal: Letters weeded clean, leaving zero backing, pre-masked under low-tack transfer paper.
  • Adhesive Styles: Standard permanent adhesive (removable without residue).
