Premium Textiles For Custom Finishes

A common mistake in custom printing is selecting cheap, low-grade blanks. Rough fabrics pill up, shrink, and result in flat, dull ink deposits. At Reverse Screen Print, we partner with top apparel brands like **Independent Trading Co., AS Colour, Lane Seven, Bella + Canvas, and Next Level** to provide retail-ready substrates.

We guide you through choosing fabric composition (100% cotton vs. poly-cotton blends vs. athletic meshes), fabric weights (from lightweight 4.3oz summer tees to heavyweight 14oz cross-grain streetwear fleece), and silhouettes. We also provide **re-labeling services** such as sewing custom woven neck tags or printing neck labels inside the collars.

Sourcing Brands:

  • Lane Seven & AS Colour: Premium midweight & heavyweight streetwear blanks.
  • Independent Trading Co: The gold standard in heavyweight fleece hoodies and zip-ups.
  • Yupoong Flexfit & Richardson: Industry-standard snapbacks, dad caps, and mesh trucker hats.

Blank Apparel Styling

Apparel Blank Guidelines

Fabric Classifications

  • Combed Ringspun Cotton: Long cotton fibers tightly spun, offering an ultra-smooth, low-fuzz printing surface.
  • CVC (Chief Value Cotton): 60/40 cotton-poly blend. Combines cotton breathability with polyester durability and low shrinkage.
  • Poly Fleece: Heavyweight brushed interiors. Ideal for keeping warm while holding high-opacity hoodie prints.
  • Sublimated Polyester: Synthetic performance fabric. Treated with dye migration blockers during curing.

Catalog Weights

  • Lightweight (4.0oz - 4.5oz): Soft, retail fitted t-shirts (e.g. Next Level 3600).
  • Midweight (5.3oz - 6.0oz): Durable, standard boxy fitting streetwear tees (e.g. Gildan 5000, AS Colour Staple).
  • Heavyweight (6.5oz - 7.5oz): Thick, structured oversized t-shirts.
  • Heavyweight Fleece (10oz - 14oz): Thick streetwear hoodies (e.g. Independent Trading Co. IND5000).
