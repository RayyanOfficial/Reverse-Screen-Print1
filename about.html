Behind the Scenes

Who We Are

Reverse Screen Print is a custom-apparel printing and graphic shop built to break stereotypes. We combine bold neon color ways with industrial print engineering.

The Story

Reversing The Status Quo

We started with a simple observation: most local printing shops feel old, boring, and outdated. They rely on standard templates, standard colors, and standard fabrics, resulting in bland merchandise that lives in the back of people's closets.

Reverse Screen Print was founded to flip the script. We wanted to build a print shop that felt alive, modern, and technical. By pairing advanced automatic textile presses with custom ink formulations and bold, high-opacity graphic designs, we create garments that people actively want to wear.

Whether it's the neon glow of our prints or the durability of our stitchwork, our mission is to make your custom merchandise the best looking item in the room.

The Workflow

Our Printing Process

From vector coordinates to final heat curing, here is how we print the magic.

Step 01

Digital Separation & Film

Your vector artwork is imported and broken down into individual color separation channels. Each color channel is outputted onto high-contrast transparent films with micro-alignment registration marks.

Step 02

Screen Emulsion Exposure

Aluminum frames are coated with light-sensitive photo emulsion. Films are taped down, and screens are loaded into our high-intensity UV exposure vacuum tables. Unexposed emulsion is washed away, creating a perfect stencil.

Step 03

Automated Ink Squeegee Run

Screens are clamped onto our automated rotary presses. Micro-adjustments are tuned until alignment is perfect. Ink is flooded, and industrial polyurethane squeegees push the paint smoothly onto the shirts.

Step 04

Conveyor Heat Cure

Printed garments are carefully placed onto our high-speed electric conveyor dryer. Heat is tracked electronically at 320°F (160°C) to cross-link the plasticizer molecules, ensuring a print that won't crack or peel.

The Machinery

Our Printing Arsenal

We invest in top-tier machinery to guarantee retail-grade accuracy and speed.

M&R Automatic Press

An all-pneumatic, high-volume automated carousel screen printing press. Capable of executing multi-color runs with precise registration at speeds over 700 garments per hour.

Multi-Head Embroidery

Industrial multi-needle embroidery machines that digitize stitch files and sew hats, bags, and premium jackets with thick thread counts and zero puckering.

HD Industrial DTF

High-definition Direct-To-Film transfer systems. We generate prints with cyan/magenta ink channel layers and white underbases, resolving fine photographic details with high flexibility.
