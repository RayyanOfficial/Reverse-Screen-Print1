Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about setups, turnaround times, or file formats? Search below or click the accordions to find answer keys.

Our minimum order size for standard screen printing is 12 items of the same design/print. However, you can mix and match garment sizes (S to 3XL) and garment colors as long as the ink colors remain identical. For orders under 12 items, we recommend our Direct-To-Film (DTF) transfers which have no minimums.
For crisp, high-opacity prints, we strongly recommend sending vector artwork. Acceptable file extensions are .AI, .EPS, .PDF, or .SVG. Ensure all text and fonts are converted to outlines. If you are uploading raster files (.PNG or .PSD), they must be transparent and at least 300 DPI at the final desired print dimensions.
Our standard production turnaround is 7 to 10 business days from the date of deposit payment and digital mockup approval. Embroidery typically takes 10 to 12 business days due to sewing file digitizing. Banners, stickers, and business cards average 4 to 6 business days.
Yes, absolutely! You can combine t-shirts, hoodies, and crewnecks in the same print run to hit your minimum quantity discounts, provided the print size and ink color stay exactly the same. If the inks require changing (e.g., swapping white ink on black shirts to black ink on white shirts), a small $15 ink-change fee applies.
Yes, we offer rush orders for urgent projects. 5-day turnaround is available for a 20% rush fee, and 3-day turnaround is available for a 40% rush fee. Please note that rush orders depend on our current press schedule and inventory availability. Contact us directly to confirm before placing an order.
Direct-To-Film (DTF) uses specialized digital printers to transfer full-color graphics onto a polyester PET film, which is then heat-pressed onto garments. Unlike Screen Printing, which requires separate stencils for each color, DTF has no screen setup fees and handles photorealistic details/gradients. Screen printing, however, is faster and more cost-effective for bulk runs (24+ items).
Yes! We use specialized high-profile framing hoops to embroider structured snapbacks, dad hats, and winter knit beanies. We digitize your flat vector logo into custom stitch coordinates and support flat embroidery, 3D puff embroidery, and custom sewn-on patches.
Due to the extensive labor and setup involved in exposing screens, mixing inks, and dialing in automatic presses, we do not print single custom screen printing samples. However, we send complete digital PDF mockups with sizing scales before printing. For large orders (144+), we can arrange physical pre-production samples upon request.
