Premium Business Cards

Make an unforgettable first impression. We print heavy-duty 16pt cardstocks finished with soft-touch velvet laminates, spot UV highlights, and metallic foils.

Description

Tactile Specialty Card Printing

A business card is a physical representation of your brand identity. At Reverse Screen Print, we do not print thin, flimsy cards. We specialize in heavyweight cardstocks (starting at **16pt thick** up to **32pt sandwich boards** with colored middle cores).

We offer premium finishing treatments: **Soft-Touch Velvet Lamination** for a smooth, peach-fuzz feel; **Raised Spot UV** which layers a glossy clear varnish over specific graphic elements (logos, patterns) to create a striking matte-to-gloss contrast; and **Metallic Foil Stamping** in gold, silver, and hot pink/magenta.

Premium Finishes Available:

  • Velvet Soft-Touch: Heavy lamination giving a premium suede texture.
  • Raised Spot UV: 50% high-gloss varnish layer standing proud off matte cardstocks.
  • Metallic Foil Stamp: Hot pressed foils available in Gold, Silver, Cyan, and Magenta.

Business Card Specs

Cardstock Standards

  • 16pt Heavyweight: Standard heavy premium cover board. Rigid feel.
  • 18pt Silk Matte: Silky laminated finish, thicker and waterproof.
  • 32pt Sandwich Board: Double-pasted cardstock containing a colored middle core (available in Cyan and Magenta cores).
  • Plastic Cards: Waterproof PVC cards (available in transparent clear or solid frosted).

Mask File Requirements

  • Varnish Mask: Vector mask layer filled with 100% Solid K (Black) to map gloss overlays.
  • Foil Mask: Separate vector mask layer defining metallic stamping paths.
  • Resolution Minimum: 350 DPI printing plates output.
  • Bleeds Margin: Standard 0.125" bleed border beyond cut lines.
