Decoration Comparison

Confused between Screen Printing, Embroidery, and DTF? Here is how they stack up.

Feature Screen Printing Custom Embroidery DTF Printing
Ideal Quantities 24+ items (Bulk discounts) 12+ items 1 to 24 items (Low minimums)
